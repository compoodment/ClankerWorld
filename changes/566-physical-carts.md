# Physical handcarts

The Blacksmith can make a handcart from wood, iron fittings and rope. An adult
collects the actual finished cart from its workshop, pulls it through the world,
loads real goods or whole vessels, and unloads into accessible storage or their
own hands. Parked carts keep their cargo and each item's owner. Cargo does not
count as a person's hand load, and vessel contents count toward cart capacity.

Carts wear while moving. One wood and one iron repair the same cart at its
location without replacing cargo. Broken carts can be left while repair supplies
are collected. A parked cart can be given to a nearby living recipient after
other owners' goods and reserved uses have been withdrawn. Spoiled cargo can be
put on nearby clear ground without deleting it or changing its ownership.

Saves preserve cart identity, position, puller, condition and actual cargo.
The map shows a temporary functional marker and reports cargo when inspected;
final vehicle art is separate work.

The recipe, capacity of 128 units, wear and travel timing are provisional.
Automated behavior checks and a hands-on game playtest are still pending.
