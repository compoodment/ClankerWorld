- Healthy, warm agents carrying a light load can swim wider rivers and lakes, moving much more slowly than wading and losing warmth. Swimming has distinct water motion on the map and survives save/reload. Cold, ill or heavily loaded agents do not start a swim, and someone already swimming can still reach shore if their condition changes. Carts and animals keep their existing movement rules; the sea still requires a boat. Speed, warmth loss and the four-unit load limit are provisional for playtesting.
