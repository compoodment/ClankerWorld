The Blacksmith now makes wooden, stone and iron axes and pickaxes, hoes,
hammers, sickles and knives. Stone needs a wooden pickaxe, iron ore needs a
stone pickaxe, and gold and diamonds need an iron pickaxe. Better axes and
pickaxes gather more per use. Deposits run out, and loose fallen wood can be
collected by hand to replace the last broken wooden tool.

Tools wear through use. Worn tools can be repaired at their household's
Blacksmith with its on-site materials; broken tools need replacing. Hammers
help construction and repairs, and knives speed food and clothing preparation.
The agent card and building storage show tool condition and broken quantities.
Costs, yields, speeds and wear rates are provisional playtest values. Hoes and
sickles also supply the field-work capabilities used by the separate farming
change. Existing tool icons are used where present; other items use the existing
crate fallback.

The expanded Blacksmith package and fallen-wood generation change content and
map identity. Earlier worlds may be refused under the alpha save policy; their
files are kept. Current tool condition, extraction and production reservations
survive saving and loading.
