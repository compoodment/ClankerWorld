- Town Councils can vote on a supported default estate law giving the Town a stated share when a resident dies without a valid will. Only later deaths use the rule, and valid wills keep priority. When no household heir survives, goods now belong to the deceased resident's actual Town, entering its Warehouse while space allows and remaining available through normal abandoned-Town salvage. Books, maps and other belongings keep their identity and quantities. Older alpha saves are refused and preserved.
