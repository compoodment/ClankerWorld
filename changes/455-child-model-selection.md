- A newborn keeps the model chosen by its parents, with the parent who started their family plan breaking a disagreement. Infants make no model calls. After infancy, a child uses that model only when its own saved key is available and normal call checks allow it; otherwise built-in choices keep the child safe, with no paid fallback. The owner can change the child's personal model later, or explicitly leave the child without one. That choice is saved; a child without a personal model continues on built-in rules and never borrows a world default.
