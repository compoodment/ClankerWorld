Agents can make padded coats and rain cloaks at their household's Tailor Shop,
craft rope and baskets in their House, and make larger carrying sacks at the
Tailor Shop. Clothing protects an agent when worn and wears with use; household
cloth repairs it at a House or Tailor Shop. Baskets and sacks share one equipped
carrying slot. Carrying limits also count goods inside reusable vessels.
Changing to a smaller aid or removing it is refused until the agent delivers
enough cargo. Existing goods are preserved when a load exceeds the limit.
All recipe amounts, protection, wear and carrying capacities are provisional.
