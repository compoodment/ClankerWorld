Households sell actual goods stocked at their Farmhouse, Blacksmith, Tailor
Shop, Restaurant, Clinic or optional Store. Customers reserve exact barter
goods and receiving space, bring their payment to the business, and receive
their purchase personally when the seller meets them there. Payments remain
private household stock at the business until collected. Saves retain offers,
stock reservations, receiving commitments and tool-making requests.

An adult Town resident may fund and build a shared Market with a clear 10 by
12 stall plot. A household reserves a marked stall by carrying its goods
there; any Town's agents may buy explicitly offered stock. Withdrawing the
last goods or receipts releases the stall. Rejected stock can be placed on
adjacent ground without deleting its identity or contents. Customers gain no
private stock or cooking access. Costs, storage, prices and offer durations
are provisional; there is no starting currency.
