Agents can till household fields on fertile land with a hoe, plant grain, potatoes or cultivated greens, tend and harvest them, and save planting stock for the next crop. Harvests stay on the field until carried to the Farmhouse or Silo. Soil inspection describes fertility in plain words, and worked soil and crops appear on the map and overview. Orchard seeds can grow new fruit trees that produce fruit in autumn. All quantities and timings remain provisional.
