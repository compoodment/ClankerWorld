The Blacksmith refines gold ore into a separate gold item, makes gold ornaments
and can set a diamond into one. Agents can wear real ornaments, give them to a
nearby recipient or barter them when unequipped. The wearer and owner are saved;
gifts clear the donor's worn slot and preserve the actual transferred goods.

Spears, swords, shields and basic armor also have Blacksmith recipes and saved
equipment slots. Changing gear preserves old stock; worn gear can be repaired
with the household's on-site iron, and broken gear needs replacement. Combat
actions, combat wear, damage and injury rules remain unfinished. All recipe
amounts, timings, reserves and repair values are provisional.
