Household animals retain their ownership, local care and waiting products in
saves. Chickens give eggs, cows give jug-held milk and sheep give wool after
receiving actual feed, water and care. Missed care stops production and riding.
Horse permissions preserve ownership; cargo and vessel contents move with the
horse and their original owners may retrieve them after permission is revoked.
Egg and milk cooking, wool cloth, and natural hides made into leather clothing
and carrying satchels use real household stock. Animals appear as simple map
markers with care, products and cargo on the tile card. All rates are provisional.
Acquisition, breeding and mortality schedules still need decisions; no starter
herd or automatic neglect death is introduced by this change.
