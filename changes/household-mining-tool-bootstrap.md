## Household mining tools

A household short of construction stone now requests a wooden pickaxe from
another household's Blacksmith when it has no usable pick. A stocked
Blacksmith makes the missing stone pickaxe before optional supply trips when
reachable iron ore is needed. Requested tools still consume the smith's real
inputs and remain private stock until the customer brings the exact payment.

This preference stops once the household has a usable pick on the required
foot route. Tool recipes, wear, quantities and choice priorities remain
provisional playtest values.
