- The Clinic draft adds a household-held 1×2 Clinic, renewable medicinal herbs and care supplies through the normal building, gathering, carrying, production and local barter paths. A House or Tailor Shop cuts cloth into bandages; a Clinic makes medicine from herbs, wood fuel and water in a reusable jug. Recipes and costs remain provisional. This work is in [#565](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/565); merge, complete validation and the Windows playtest are still pending.

- The owner's October 2 decision keeps bandage production, storage and trade in this stage. Injury causes and bandage treatment wait for the later injury stage. Medicine uses the existing illness system; it does not instantly restore full health.

- The related [#749](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/749) draft returns empty household pots and jugs from workstations to the House through real pickup and delivery, so reusable supplies can circulate again. Ownership, reservations, carrying room and destination space still apply; runtime checks are pending.

- Medicine consumes one real dose and reduces illness gradually. Adults choose named caregivers through a fresh personal-model decision and may revoke permission. Self-care and a dependent's accepted caregiver use their existing authority. Buying supplies or sharing a household grants no private-stock or treatment access; Jev, failed replies, repeated intentions and owner orders cannot grant adult permission.

- A treatment course and its permission survive saving and reopening. Interruption stops the remaining effect without refunding the spent dose. Death releases live inventory claims while preserving completed consumption receipts. This draft uses private-world schema 44 above its parent's schema 43; older alpha saves are refused and preserved without migration.
