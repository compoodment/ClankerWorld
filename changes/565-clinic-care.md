# Clinic supplies and gradual recovery

Households can build a Clinic, gather distinct medicinal herbs, and bring herbs,
fresh water and fuel into its stock. Houses and Tailor Shops cut cloth into
bandages. Patients use their own supplies, or explicitly permit a named adult
caregiver to bring a dose to them. Accepted caregiver relationships also permit
care for their dependents. Other households' Clinic stock requires a purchase.

A dose is consumed once, then applied over twenty world steps. Revoking consent
or a caregiver's death stops the remaining treatment; already consumed supplies
are not recreated. Saves preserve the consumed dose and its remaining effect.
The agent card reports health, treatment, worn equipment and actual carrying load.

Costs, recipe quantities, carrying amounts and recovery rates are provisional.
Automated checks and a hands-on game playtest are still pending for this change.
