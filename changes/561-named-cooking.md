# Concrete household meals and the Restaurant

Households can cook potato or greens meals, porridge, bread and vegetable stew
from their actual House stock. Berries and fruit can enrich porridge. An
optional Restaurant cooks porridge, bread and stew, plus a more filling meal
from bread and cultivated greens. Cooked food stays at the building that made
it, and potatoes needed to replant fields stay reserved.

Water stays inside its reusable jug while carried between buildings. Cooking
uses the reserved water and leaves the jug intact. Supplies wait for room in
the destination; picking up a jug accounts for the weight of its contents and
other incoming deliveries. Missing cooking stock names the needed ingredients.

New generated Houses begin with eight meal portions each. Cooking aims for a
small household reserve, including portions already carried or being made,
leaving agents time for other activities. Foods provide their actual
nourishment when given to a child as well as when eaten by an adult.

All amounts, work times and nutrition remain provisional. The Restaurant is a
trial 2×2 building costing 16 wood and 4 stone. Recipe amounts follow the
[approved pipelines](../docs/game-design/towns.md#food-and-replanting).
Restaurant sales are connected by the separate business work in
[#564](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/564). A hands-on
playtest of cooking and pacing remains pending. Art and textures are separate.
