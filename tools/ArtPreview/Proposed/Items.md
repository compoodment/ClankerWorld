# Item icons, round 1

This is the round-1 reference set for item icons: 25 icons in all.

- **Seven icons the game already needs but lacks:** water, wild greens, potato,
  porridge, stew, meal and diamond. Today each of these shows a plain crate.
- **Six improved icons that keep their names:** wood, stone, grain, bread,
  clothing and wooden axe.
- **Twelve new icons:** stone axe, iron axe, iron pickaxe, sickle, rope,
  basket, water jug, bandage, medicine, map, book and coin.

Each icon is drawn the same way as the current ones: 16 × 16 pixels, typed
out as rows of letters, with an empty one-pixel border and the game's own dark
outline added around the shape. They are meant to sit beside the current
icons, not replace their look. I checked them on the parchment panel colour at
full size (what players see) and at 4×, mixed in with the current icons.

## Rules from the style guide

- **I1, shading:** three or four shades per material, all taken from the style
  guide's colour ramps. Light comes from the top-left and shade falls to the
  bottom-right.
- **I2, telling icons apart:** every icon has its own shape and colour on
  parchment. The pairs most likely to be confused were checked side by side:
  - stone and iron ore
  - rope and fiber
  - bandage and cloth
  - the three bowls and plates
  - the three axes
- **I3, tool tiers:** all tools share one handle and differ in the head.
  - The wooden head is pale carved wood.
  - The stone head is grey knapped stone.
  - The iron head is bright steel with a one-pixel glint.
  - The wooden and stone heads are lashed on with fiber cord. The iron head
    has a socket around the handle.
- **I4, cooked food:**
  - porridge sits in a wooden bowl
  - stew sits in a clay bowl
  - the meal sits on a plate
  - bread stays a loaf
- **L3, outline:** every icon gets the game's complete outline and keeps an
  empty border.

## Icon by icon

**Food and drink**

- **Water:** a blue drop with a highlight. It is the only drop-shaped icon, so
  it cannot be mistaken for the jug.
- **Wild greens:** a loose bunch of three pointed leaves with pale midribs. It
  reads as picked leaves, which leaves the round lettuce shape free for
  cultivated greens later.
- **Potato:** two lumpy tan potatoes with eyes, a big one behind and a small
  one in front.
- **Grain (improved):** a fuller sheaf of three plump ears, with a solid
  bundle of stalks tied by a band. The current icon's thin stalks are busy
  once outlined.
- **Bread (improved):** a taller, domed loaf with three diagonal cuts showing
  pale crumb. It keeps the current crust colours.
- **Porridge:** a wooden bowl heaped with cream porridge, with a spoon.
- **Stew:** a clay bowl with small side handles, holding brown broth with
  potato and green pieces.
- **Meal:** two potatoes and a heap of greens on a pale plate. This matches the
  agreed simple meal of potatoes and greens.

**Materials**

- **Wood (improved):** the same two logs. The ends are now rounder and paler,
  with a growth ring and a centre dot, and the bark has short grooves instead
  of scattered specks.
- **Stone (improved):** a faceted chunk with a flat lit top, a crease and a
  separate pebble. It uses cool light greys, so it no longer has the same
  shape and nearly the same colour as iron ore.
- **Diamond:** a cut gem with a flat top, facets and a point, in the diamond
  blues.

**Tools**

- **Wooden axe (improved):** a flared blade of pale carved wood, lashed to the
  handle. It follows rule I3: the current wooden axe has a grey, stone-looking
  head.
- **Stone axe:** a grey stone head with rounded, chipped corners, lashed on.
- **Iron axe:** a flared steel blade with a socket and a glint.
- **Iron pickaxe:** the current wooden pickaxe's layout with a steel head.
- **Sickle:** a curved steel blade, bright along its inner cutting edge, on
  the shared handle.

**Crafted goods**

- **Rope:** a coil seen from above, with two turns, dark gaps between them and
  a frayed loose end. A plain loop with a tail read as a magnifying glass, and
  a filled coil read as a ring loaf.
- **Basket:** woven wicker with an arched handle you can see through.
- **Water jug:** a clay jug with a looped handle and a glimpse of water in the
  mouth. Its colour matches the clay icon.
- **Clothing (improved):** the same blue tunic, now lit on the left and shaded
  on the right, with a V neck, dark cuffs, a belt with a buckle and a hem.
- **Bandage:** a cream cloth roll with its spiral end showing and the loose
  strip unrolling.
- **Medicine:** a corked glass bottle of red tincture with a cream label and a
  green cross. I used green rather than red because the red cross is a
  protected emblem.
- **Map:** a three-panel folded map on sand-coloured paper, showing land, a
  river and a red route to an X.
- **Book:** a closed red book with a small gold emblem and cream page edges.
- **Coin:** a gold coin tilted to show its edge, with a raised rim and a small
  star.

## Choices you may want to change

1. **Iron tools use the steel of the iron bar icon, not the style guide's
   Iron ramp.** The guide's Iron and Rock ramps are almost the same warm grey,
   so stone and iron tools would look alike. I suggest the guide gets a
   "worked iron" ramp from the iron bar's colours.
2. **Tool handles keep today's handle browns** rather than the Timber ramp.
   That way the new tools match the wooden pickaxe and the generic tool icon,
   which are not redrawn in this round.
3. **The coin is gold.** The content list says agents create their own
   currencies and there is no gold standard. If gold suggests otherwise, a
   copper coin is a colour swap.
4. **Clothing adds one darker blue** for the inside of the neck. Bread keeps
   its current crust colours, because the guide has no bread ramp.

## Not done in this round

- **Wooden pickaxe:** it is not in the round-1 list, so it still has a grey
  head. Under rule I3 it should get a pale wooden head in round 2.
- **Generic tool icon:** also not in the round-1 list, so it is unchanged.
- **The reference scene:** item icons do not appear on the map, so it is
  unchanged.
- **The review sheet's background:** it shows the icons on a grey checker. On
  the game's parchment panels they look as in the checks described above.
