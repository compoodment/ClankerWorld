### Questions for you

- **Roads meeting bridges.** The new bridge deck stays inside its tile, so the game should treat the deck as Road when joining the street to it. The scene shows that rule. Without it, a strip of grass shows before each bridge.
- **Coin colour.** The coin icon is gold. Copper would avoid hinting at a gold standard, which the design rules out.
- **New colours.** The drawings add a few shades the style guide does not list yet: a clay-bank brown, a brighter worked iron for tools, a lighter outcrop stone, dark trousers seen mid-stride, and colours for the Store, Clinic and Market. Approving the direction approves these.
- **Agents need two small game changes** before the new art shows in play: the map must pass each agent's facing and what they are doing (walking, carrying, working, talking, hurt) to the sprite.
- **Depleted and picked states** such as a picked berry bush need the game to track and send them. Today one grey sprite serves every depleted site.
