# Skills and lessons

Pending for PR #577. No Windows playtest is claimed.

- Start a normal world and add an adult. Agents begin without skills, then their first completed building, farming, crafting or smithing work shows a skill on their card. Gathering alone grants none. ([#471](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/471))
- A finished House or Warehouse expansion teaches Building. Starting work or a refused or canceled attempt grants none; repeating completed work keeps the first learning time. Save and load after completion to check the card keeps the skill. ([#471](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/471))
- After someone learns a skill, watch another agent request and complete a lesson. The learner's card names the skill and teacher. A declined or cancelled lesson grants none. ([#471](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/471))
- Save and load during and after a lesson. Its progress and the learned skill remain, and the teacher's name remains readable even if the teacher later dies. ([#471](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/471))
