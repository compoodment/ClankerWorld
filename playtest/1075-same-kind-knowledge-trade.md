# Exchange records containing different discoveries

Pending a hands-on Windows check after merge.

- Have two adults scout different places and write a field record each. Bring
  them together and let them agree to barter. Check that each receives the
  other's record and learns its recorded discovery, with the original
  discoverer preserved. Save and reload, then check their records and learned
  sites again. After both have learned both records' contents, check that they
  do not keep proposing to exchange those records just to learn them again.
