# Physical cart hauling

Pending Windows hands-on playtest. Capacity, costs and wear are provisional.

- On the paired world: carry actual wood, iron and rope into a Blacksmith to make a handcart. Deploy it, load more stock than the adult can carry and add a filled jug. Pull it along Roads and ordinary ground; inspect its position, owners, jug contents, condition and movement delay. Save and reload midway; the actual cart and cargo should remain together. ([#566](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/566))
- On the paired world: park at the destination and unload into an authorized House or Warehouse with room. Loading beyond capacity, taking another household's cargo, boarding a boat while attached, and giving a cart that holds someone else's goods should be refused without losing cargo. ([#566](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/566))
- On the paired world: wear the cart to zero condition, bring one wood and one iron beside it, repair it and continue hauling. Put spoiled cargo on clear ground; the actual lots should remain there with their owner. ([#566](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/566))
