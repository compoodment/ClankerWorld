# Physical cart hauling

Pending Windows hands-on playtest. Capacity, costs and wear are provisional.

Build a Blacksmith and carry actual wood, iron and rope into it to make a handcart. Deploy the actual cart, load more stock than the adult can carry, add a filled jug, and pull it along Roads and across ordinary ground. Inspect the cart's position, cargo owners, jug contents, condition and movement delay. Save and reload midway.

Park at the destination and unload into an authorized House or Warehouse with sufficient room. Try loading beyond capacity, taking another household's cargo, boarding a boat while attached, and giving a parked cart that holds someone else's goods. These actions must be refused without losing cargo. Wear a cart to zero condition, bring one wood and one iron beside it, repair it, and continue hauling. Put spoiled cargo on clear ground; the actual lots must remain there.

Automated checks cover actual lot identity and contents, capacity, ownership, stock access, reservations, repair, spoiled cargo and replay. The visual and ordinary-choice flow still needs this hands-on check.
