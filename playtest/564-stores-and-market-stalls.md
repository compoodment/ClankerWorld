# Stores and Market stalls

Pending Windows playtests for [#564](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/564).

- On the paired world: let a household stock its Store or work building with surplus goods and offer them for barter. A customer should bring the actual requested payment and meet the seller; both goods should change hands once, without giving the customer access to private stock. ([#564](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/564))
- On the paired world: let an adult build the shared Market, then bring goods to an empty stall. The stall should remain held by that household while unsold stock or received payment is there, and become free once all its goods are physically withdrawn. ([#564](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/564))
- On the paired world: save during an accepted sale of a filled food pot, then load and complete the exchange. The pot and its real contents should arrive together, while the buyer and seller retain enough receiving space. ([#564](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/564))
- On the paired world: request a tool from a Blacksmith that lacks its inputs. The request should wait with an understandable reason, then use actual delivered materials and remain private until barter is paid. ([#564](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/564))
- On the paired world: let stale stall goods be cleared. They should appear on legal nearby ground with their ownership and contents preserved, and should no longer block the empty stall. ([#564](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/564))
- On the paired world: ask a household to withdraw its remaining unsold stall goods. An adult should collect them physically and carry them home when there is room; any remaining barter receipts should keep the stall held. ([#564](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/564))
- On the paired world: offer leather and a leather satchel at the Tailor Shop. An adult wearing a basket should recognize the satchel's larger carrying capacity and be able to buy it. ([#564](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/564))
- On the paired world: offer two books with different discoveries. Each buyer should want the actual offered book only when it contains knowledge they have not learned. ([#564](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/564))
