# Counter an unfinished remedy

Pending a hands-on Windows check after merge.

- Let two adults renegotiate an unfinished remedy involving real goods. Have
  one counter the new offer, then have every contributor read and accept those
  exact terms. Save and reload before they perform the returns. Check that only
  the replacement's remaining contributions are performed, with no additional
  payment under the old agreement. Repeat with a second counteroffer and check
  that the original agreement and countered offers remain visible in history.
