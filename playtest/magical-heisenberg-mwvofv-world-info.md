# World Info pages

Pending for the World Info pull request. No Windows playtest is claimed.

- Open World Info on its World page in both themes. The top card shows today's date, the season and the weather where the camera is, and the eight tiles count agents, Towns, households, buildings, road tiles, bridges, resource sites and the map size. Pan to another weather region; the card follows the camera.
- Open the Towns page in a world with a Town. Each Town shows its residents' portraits with their names on hover, and **Show** moves the map to it. There is no line about Town borders.
- Below the Town list, households show their stores as item slots with counts, and projects show the worker's portrait, a progress bar and any blocker in red.
- In a busy world, the Town details scroll inside World Info instead of running off the bottom of the screen, with a gap before the scrollbar.
