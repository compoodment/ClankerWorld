- On the paired world: have one Blacksmith household member sell the household's only iron or gold at a borrowed Market stall. Offer the same metal from another household's stall, and give a housemate personal goods to pay with. With both trading adults willing, the housemate can purchase needed metal while the household's original sale stock stays intact. After the first seller leaves, the household's released stock is collectible and prevents redundant purchases. ([#1361](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/1361))
