- Start a new world and choose a Town site. The first Town follows a winding main road with side streets, and its two Houses, Warehouse, Farmhouse and Blacksmith sit along them. ([#525](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/525))
- Try a few different sites before placing founders. Streets bend in 45° steps and take diagonals where the land is clear, no two streets run side by side, and no Road runs under a building. ([#525](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/525))
- Look at where each street ends. It carries on about three tiles past the last building on it, and no side street is left without a building. ([#525](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/525))
- Look at each building in the first Town. Its door is drawn on the side facing its Road, so doors face different ways around the Town, and they stay on the same side after save and load. ([#523](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/523))
- Roads draw as one joined packed-dirt track, with rounded bends, smooth diagonals and no crosshatch where Road tiles meet. A short doorstep path joins each door to its Road. ([#524](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/524))
- Find Roads on sand or snow, then zoom right out. Roads keep a darker edge on pale ground, and at overview zoom they still show as clear lines. ([#524](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/524))
- Turn on the Town borders filter. The border follows the Town's shape with about three tiles of spare land around every building and Road, leaves out water, and is the same after save and load. ([#526](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/526))
- Open Filters at the start of a session. Both start off. Turn on Town borders: they draw as a pale dashed line that reads on grass, forest, sand and snow, and as a thin solid line at overview zoom. ([#542](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/542))
- Open Add Agent and point at land. Town borders and household property show while you place, without turning Filters on, and go away when the panel closes. The World Info Towns hint and the Filters tooltip describe the dashed line. ([#542](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/542))
- Let the world run until a household builds something new, such as the Farmhouse or Blacksmith it lacks, a Silo or a Tailor Shop. A new Town building takes a free spot facing an existing Road where there is one. ([#527](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/527))
- A new building away from the Roads gets a new side street to the nearest Road, and streets run on about three tiles past the newest building. The border grows so that every Town Road stays inside it. ([#527](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/527))
- Watch agents who keep wading across the same one-tile river. After six crossings by at least two agents within two world days, a plank bridge appears and the Event Log says agents crossed there so often that a bridge was built. ([#460](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/460))
- Hover over and click a bridge. The hover readout and tile card say Bridge, World Info counts it, and agents walk across it at normal speed. ([#460](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/460))
- When a Town grows beside a river, a new street may cross a river up to two tiles wide on a bridge. Wider rivers, lakes and the sea are never bridged, and no second bridge is built over the same stretch of river. Note whether bridges for short streets past a door turn up too often. ([#460](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/460))
