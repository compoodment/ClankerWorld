# Paper, maps and books

Pending Windows hands-on playtest. Paper and binding costs and work time are provisional.

- On the paired world: gather fiber, fill a real water jug and carry both into a household House. Make paper, saving and reloading during the work. The finished paper should remain in the House, the water should be consumed once and the jug should survive. ([#207](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/207))
- On the paired world: have an adult explore and write the learned sites at their House with paper. A record contains one site; a map or book contains up to nine. A book also needs a cloth cover. Distant supplies should be collected and delivered before writing can finish. ([#207](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/207))
- On the paired world: transfer a written item by an agreed barter or Store purchase. Only its new reader should learn its contents, with the original discoverer preserved. Leave another inhabitant nearby and check that the knowledge does not spread to them. ([#207](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/207))
- On the paired world: store a book at another household building, have its household reader collect it and deliver paper and cloth to their House, then copy it. The copy should preserve the real sites and first discoverer, consume actual writing supplies and retain its source after save and reload. ([#207](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/207))
