- On the paired world: make two saves a few days apart, load the first, play a day and open **Save World**. The timeline shows branch 1 with both saves, a banner on the newer one, and **You are here** on a dotted **New branch** row bending down from the loaded save. The card underneath says your new save starts Branch 2. ([#680](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/680))
- On the paired world: save from the previous check, then open **Load Save** from Load World. Branch 2 bends down from the save it grew from, each branch's newest save has a small banner in its colour, autosaves are small diamonds, and the season bar names the seasons along the top. **You are here** follows Branch 2's newest save. ([#680](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/680))
- On the paired world: in Load Save, click a save. Its name tag turns orange with corner marks, and the card underneath names its branch, its date and whether playing on from it starts a new branch. Double-click a save, or press Enter after choosing it with the arrow keys, and the load confirmation opens. ([#680](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/680))
- On the paired world: in Save World, click an autosave and then a named save. The autosave can't be chosen; the named save can, and **Overwrite** asks to replace it. ([#680](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/680))
- On the paired world: switch Load Save and Save World to **List** and back. The same save stays chosen, and the list shows the saves as cards grouped by branch. ([#680](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/680))
- On the paired world: in a world with four or more branches, open Load Save and Save World at the 1080p UI size. Each panel fits on screen, the timeline scrolls up and down for branches that don't fit, and the branch names on the left and the season bar stay in view while it scrolls. Say whether Save World, which shows about two branches at once, feels too cramped. ([#680](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/680))
- On the paired world: switch the theme to dark and open Load Save. The branch colours, banners, season bar and **You are here** marker are all easy to see. ([#680](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/680))
