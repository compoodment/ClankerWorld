# Building expansion and storm guests

Pending for PR #572. No Windows playtest is claimed.

- Let a House or Warehouse become nearly full. If an agent expands it, the same building grows, retains its stock and owner, and Details shows its larger footprint and storage. ([#463](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/463))
- Save and load while expansion or cooking is underway. Work resumes and the original stock stays in that building. ([#463](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/463))
- Watch an invited guest shelter in a House during a storm. The guest gets shelter, and cooking and private stock remain for household members. After an adult household member revokes the invitation, the guest loses that shelter access. ([#463](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/463))
- Watch a helper from another household deliver building materials. The materials reach the receiving House, or stay carried if there is no House, without giving the helper access to private stock. ([#463](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/463))
