# Concrete household and Restaurant meals

Pending hands-on Windows checks for [#561](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/561).

- On a disposable generated world, follow harvested grain through a carried load into a Farmhouse, then flour carried into the household House and bread cooked with fresh water and wood. Food should appear only where it was carried or cooked. ([#561](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/561))
- Watch simple potato or greens meals, porridge and stew being prepared. Missing water or wood should prevent the work and say which ingredient is needed. A used jug should remain available to collect and refill. ([#561](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/561))
- Let a household with a House and Restaurant share one jug. An available meal should be cooked before repeated stock movements; Restaurant meals should use bread and greens and improve fullness and diet. A different household should have no access to the private stock or cooking. ([#561](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/561))
- Pause, save and reload while ingredients are being carried and while cooking is underway. The same loads, vessel contents, stock and completed servings should remain, and meal names and stock icons should be clear in building and agent panels. ([#561](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/561))
- Let the world run through several meals. Cooking, carrying and eating should leave agents time for conversation, building and exploration. Grain should keep longer than flour, bread and cooked meals, with pots slowing spoilage. Record balance problems as playtest feedback. ([#561](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/561))
