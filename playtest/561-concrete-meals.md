# Concrete household and Restaurant meals

Pending hands-on Windows checks for [#561](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/561).

- On a disposable generated world, follow harvested grain through a carried load into a Farmhouse, then flour carried into the household House and bread cooked with fresh water and wood. Food should appear only where it was carried or cooked. ([#561](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/561))
- Watch simple potato or greens meals, porridge and stew being prepared. Missing water or wood should prevent the work and say which ingredient is needed. A used jug should remain available to collect and refill. ([#561](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/561))
- Let a household with a House and Restaurant share one jug. Each building should keep the ingredients it needs, including water inside a jug, so stock does not shuttle back and forth. Restaurant meals should use bread and greens and improve fullness and diet. A different household should have no access to the private stock or cooking. ([#561](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/561))
- Pause, save and reload while ingredients are being carried and while cooking is underway. The same loads, vessel contents, stock and completed servings should remain, and meal names and stock icons should be clear in building and agent panels. ([#561](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/561))
- Let the world run through several meals. Cooking, carrying and eating should leave agents time for conversation, building and exploration. Raw grain, potatoes and flour should keep their freshness; bread and cooked meals should spoil, with pots slowing that loss. Record balance problems as playtest feedback. ([#561](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/561))
- Ask an agent to eat a named meal while other food is nearby. The order should use that meal, wait when it is unavailable or claimed, and resume correctly after saving and loading. ([#561](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/561))
- Let consenting parents prepare for a child with enough separate two-serving cooked meal lots. Birth should use four real servings and leave the household's food reserve intact. ([#561](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/561))
- In cold weather, watch an adult fuel the House and then cook at the Restaurant. The Restaurant should spend its wood on the recipe and should not gain a heating fire. ([#561](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/561))
