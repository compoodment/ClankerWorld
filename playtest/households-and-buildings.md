# Households and buildings

Merged on 30 September from [#543](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/543), [#441](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/441), [#470](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/470) and [#469](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/469).

- Click a House, the Farmhouse, the Blacksmith and the Warehouse. Each is outlined, and a card opens beside it with its roof, name and owner, one status line (what is being made, with a progress bar and time left, or who is inside), and stored items as icons with the count in a corner badge. ([#543](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/543))
- Press Details. A panel docks on the left with the owner, who may use the building, when it was built, which side its door faces, work in progress, a larger storage grid with item names, and who is inside or lives there. Check these against what the agents are doing. ([#543](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/543))
- Leave Details open while agents store or take items. It updates as they do. Back or Escape returns to the card, and × closes it. ([#543](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/543))
- Switch between the Light and Dark themes and try a larger UI Scale. The card and Details fit on screen, scrolling where needed, and the count badges stay visible. ([#543](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/543))
- Run a new world for a couple of days. The household holding the Farmhouse grows grain and mills flour there. The household holding the Blacksmith makes wooden axes and pickaxes, and mines iron ore if an outcrop can be reached. ([#441](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/441))
- Watch who works where. Agents never work at a building that another household holds. ([#441](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/441))
- Each household gathers the materials it is missing and builds the productive building it lacks: the Farmhouse household a Blacksmith, the Blacksmith household a Farmhouse. Click the new building: its owner is the household that built it. ([#470](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/470))
- Each household holding a Farmhouse then builds one Silo, a round building with a cone roof, within two tiles of it. Its harvests other than ready-to-eat food are stored there. ([#470](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/470))
- No household builds a second building of the same kind, and none builds a Warehouse or other building the whole Town shares. Watch whether building work leaves agents hungry: in one offline run an adult got close to the urgent hunger line. ([#470](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/470))
- At the start of a new world, check each House card. It holds one garment for each starting agent who lives there, and the Town has no Weaving frame. ([#469](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/469))
- Let the world run. Each household builds a Tailor Shop (purple, with a thread-spool sign), brings in plant fiber, weaves cloth and sews clothing, and an agent without clothing collects a garment from the shop and wears it. ([#469](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/469))
- Before the first Tailor Shop is built, or while a shop has no fiber, nobody makes cloth or clothing and nothing says they did. ([#469](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/469))
