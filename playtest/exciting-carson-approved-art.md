# Approved art in the game

From the October 1 art review, on the branch `claude/exciting-carson-u13ktz`.

- Start a new world and zoom from the closest view out to mid zoom. Grass, forest, sand, rock, snow and tundra should look calm, with no visible tile grid, and the art should switch cleanly to the smaller mid-zoom tiles.
- Look at a river with a bridge. The street should run right onto the plank deck, with no strip of grass between the Road and the bridge.
- Watch an agent walk in different directions, including diagonally. They should face the way they move, step while moving, and stand still about a second and a half after stopping.
- Watch agents carry goods to a building, harvest or mine, trade or talk, and fall ill. Each should show the matching pose while doing it.
- Open a House and the Warehouse building card. Food, water and tools should show their own icons, not crates.
