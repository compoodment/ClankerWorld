# Mountain massifs

Waiting for computment's map review after [#683](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/683). The massif counts, minimum size and hill widths are provisional until this review.

- Preview several Small and Medium Balanced worlds, wrapped and not wrapped. Each should show one or two (Small) or two to four (Medium) long mountain ranges that read as whole landforms, with no small stray mountain patches. Say whether the number and size of ranges look right. ([#683](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/683))
- Look at a range up close: the peaks should form a line along its middle with walkable mountain all around them, and the hills around it should be wider around bigger ranges. Say whether the hills reach too far or not far enough. ([#683](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/683))
- Start a world and check that agents can reach stone near the first Town and walk around a range rather than being blocked by its peaks. ([#683](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/683))
- Try Low and High mountain settings and a world with 80% water: Low should have small ranges, High large ones, and an island world should still have ranges on its larger islands. ([#683](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/683))
- On the paired world: try to load a world made before this change. It should be listed as unable to load, and its save should still be there. ([#683](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/683))
