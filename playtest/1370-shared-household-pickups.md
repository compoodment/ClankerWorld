- In a paired world, let an adult gather wood or stone using a shared household tool from its House. The adult walks to collect the actual tool, keeps its household owner, and can continue work after save/reload. Also ask an adult to plant a tree using a household seed delivered to the House; the real seed is collected and used once. ([#1370](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/1370))
- In a paired world with a household cow and Animal Yard, keep feed and a water jug in household stock. Ask an adult to care for the cow with limited free carrying space: pickup waits when the whole jug cannot fit, then succeeds after room is freed. Save and reload during the supply trip; the same jug reaches the cow, care spends real feed and water, and the vessel keeps its household owner. ([#1370](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/1370))
