# Leaving and starting a household

Pending Windows hands-on checks for [#593](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/593).

- On the paired world: ask an adult's model to leave their household. Inspect their membership, goods awaiting collection and housing need. The old House, shared stock and Town membership remain recorded. ([#593](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/593))
- Store a personal coat in the House and borrow a household work tool before leaving. Collect the coat physically in limited loads, return the borrowed tool, save and reload mid-move. The coat stays personal, the tool stays household-owned and the food allowance does not repeat. ([#593](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/593))
- Have a caregiver leave with dependent children, then try a destination with insufficient places. The group stays together, care continues physically and the real housing need stays visible. Try an adult explicitly accepting replacement care before another departure. ([#593](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/593))
- Have a homeless adult seek existing homes and receive refusals. They can then form a household alone and pursue a House; no free building or materials appear. ([#593](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/593))
- Leave during private crafting or House expansion, save and reload, then have a remaining member resume at the worksite. The original materials, owner, completed work and reserved space stay recorded. ([#593](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/593))
