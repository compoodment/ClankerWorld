# Reusable pots and water jugs

The automated checks cover these paths, but they still need trying in the Windows game.

- In a new world, let a household build a House, dig clay and make a storage pot and water jug. Check that the House lists them by name with different icons. ([#560](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/560))
- Let household food go into the pot, then let a hungry adult take a serving from it. Check that the pot remains and the adult can eat the food. ([#560](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/560))
- Let an adult collect the House's jug, fill it at a reachable riverbank or lakeshore, and return it to the House. Save and reopen the world; check that the jug and its water are still there. ([#560](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/560))
