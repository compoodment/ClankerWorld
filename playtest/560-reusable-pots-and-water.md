# Reusable pots and water jugs

The automated checks cover these paths, but they still need trying in the Windows game.

- In a new world, let a household build a House, dig clay and make a storage pot and water jug. Check that the House lists them by name with different icons. ([#560](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/560))
- Let household food go into the pot, then let a hungry adult take a serving from it. Check that the pot remains and the adult can eat the food. ([#560](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/560))
- Let an adult collect the House's jug, fill it at a reachable riverbank or lakeshore, and return it to the House. Save and reopen the world; check that the jug and its water are still there. ([#560](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/560))
- Let a hungry adult carry a filled jug with too little room for food. Check that they set down the jug with its water, using the camp pile when the House cannot fit the whole load, then gather food. Save and reopen; the water must stay in its jug. ([#560](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/560))
- Fill an adult's load with supplies while the household's only food is in a pot. Check that they set down enough supplies to take a serving, including after reopening the save. ([#560](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/560))
- Leave just enough House space for a delivery already on its way. Check that another adult's jug or spare food does not take that space, and that an adult across disconnected land is not repeatedly sent to store food there. ([#560](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/560))
