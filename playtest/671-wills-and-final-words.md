# Wills and final words

Waiting for a hands-on check after [#671](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/671).

- On the paired world, when an agent with a personal model dies owning belongings, open their profile: within a few seconds it should say who the will names and what each heir is left, or that the household inherits, followed by any final words. The words should read naturally and stay short. ([#671](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/671))
- After the estate's seven-day hold ends, open each heir's Memories. Everyone who inherited should remember "Name's final words were: '…'", and agents who inherited nothing should not. If the will named the Town, its share should appear in the Town Warehouse, and any food meant for the Town should go to the household instead. ([#671](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/671))
- Save and reopen the world between the death and the end of the hold. The profile should show the same will, and the estate should still be divided as written, without asking the model again. ([#671](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/671))
