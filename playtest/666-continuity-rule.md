# Continuity rule

Prepared for [#666](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/666).
These Windows checks remain pending; automated checks do not replace them.

- Create a new world with four founders. The Event Log should say the continuity rule is on, once, and say it again only if the world later drops back below eight people who are not elders. Save and reopen: the line should not repeat. ([#666](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/666))
- Let two founders become partners. When one asks for a child, the other should only be able to say "not yet", not refuse. About two in-game days after they became partners, their plan should go ahead even if neither agrees, and the birth should follow once their household has food and shelter. ([#666](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/666))
- Add agents until eight people who are not elders are alive. The Event Log should say the rule is off, and a partner should be able to decline a child again. ([#666](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/666))
