# Civic and business inspection

Pending Windows playtests for the owner's content request and
[approved Town rules](../docs/game-design/towns.md#council-size-and-terms-approved-on-october-1).

- On the paired world: open the Town panel while a Store offers goods or a Market stall has stock. The panel should name the seller, goods, exact payment and pending meetings, without exposing internal identifiers. ([#564](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/564))
- On the paired world: open the Town panel during an election or rule vote. It should show that Town's council, progress, laws and term end, without showing the old household council as the Town's authority. ([Town rules](../docs/game-design/towns.md#council-size-and-terms-approved-on-october-1))
- On the paired world: read the Event Log after an election, business exchange, cart delivery or medical treatment. Names and descriptions should be readable, including agents descended from the founders. ([Town rules](../docs/game-design/towns.md#council-size-and-terms-approved-on-october-1))
