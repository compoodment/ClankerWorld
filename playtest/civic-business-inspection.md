# Civic and business inspection

Pending Windows playtests for the owner's content request and
[approved Town rules](../docs/game-design/towns.md#election-terms-and-council-continuity).

- On the paired world: open the Town panel while a Store offers goods or a Market stall has stock. The panel should name the seller, goods, exact payment and pending meetings, without exposing internal identifiers. ([#564](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/564))
- On the paired world: open the Town panel while reading the continuing candidate register, a regular election, a replacement, a runoff and a rule vote. It should name the willing nominees and already selected councillors, distinguish the runoff, show the available seats, deadline, laws and term end, and explain fallback councils and recorded draws. ([Town rules](../docs/game-design/towns.md#election-terms-and-council-continuity))
- On the paired world: read the Event Log after an election, business exchange, cart delivery or medical treatment. Names and descriptions should be readable, including agents descended from the founders. ([Town rules](../docs/game-design/towns.md#election-terms-and-council-continuity))
