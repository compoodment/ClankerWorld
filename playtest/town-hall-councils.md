# Town Hall councils

Pending Windows playtests for the owner's October 1 request and
[approved Town rules](../docs/game-design/towns.md#council-size-and-terms-approved-on-october-1).
This direct owner request has no separate Implementation issue.

- On the paired world: let an adult build the 3×4 Town Hall from actual wood and stone. It should belong to the Town, with residents able to approach it from several sides. ([Town rules](../docs/game-design/towns.md#council-size-and-terms-approved-on-october-1))
- On the paired world: let an adult propose a rule at the Hall, then cast an explicit vote. Strictly more than half the current council must approve; a tie keeps the existing rule. A communal-food rule should leave each household able to collect its own food. ([Town rules](../docs/game-design/towns.md#council-size-and-terms-approved-on-october-1))
- On the paired world: grow one Town to eight adults and watch a full day of voluntary nominations and voting. Voters may support themselves, revise their choices or abstain, and should resume ordinary work after responding without having to volunteer. Only willing nominees who receive a vote may take one of the three seats for a game year. Children and residents of another Town have no vote. ([Town rules](../docs/game-design/towns.md#council-size-and-terms-approved-on-october-1))
- On the paired world: arrange a tie for the last seat. The Hall should show a one-day runoff between the tied nominees, keeping the already selected members. A remaining tie or too few supported nominees leaves vacancies. ([Town rules](../docs/game-design/towns.md#council-size-and-terms-approved-on-october-1))
- On the paired world: save during nominations, revised voting and a runoff, then load and continue. Departed or deceased people should lose their candidacy, vote and seat; newcomers vote in the next election. A changed council cancels an unfinished law vote so it can be proposed again. Starting an annual election under the same councillors should preserve that law vote. ([Town rules](../docs/game-design/towns.md#council-size-and-terms-approved-on-october-1))
- On the paired world: approach a Hall in a boat. Passengers should land before proposing, nominating or voting, even when the boat is beside the Hall. ([Town rules](../docs/game-design/towns.md#council-size-and-terms-approved-on-october-1))
