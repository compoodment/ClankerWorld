# Town Hall councils

Pending Windows playtests for the owner's October 1 request and
[approved Town rules](../docs/game-design/towns.md#election-terms-and-council-continuity), settled in #617 and implemented under #618.

- On the paired world: let an adult build the 3×4 Town Hall from actual wood and stone. It should belong to the Town, with residents able to approach it from several sides. ([Town rules](../docs/game-design/towns.md#election-terms-and-council-continuity))
- On the paired world: let an adult propose a rule at the Hall, then cast an explicit vote. Strictly more than half the current council must approve; a tie keeps the existing rule. A communal-food rule should leave each household able to collect its own food. ([Town rules](../docs/game-design/towns.md#election-terms-and-council-continuity))
- On the paired world: keep a continuing register of willing adults, then grow one Town to eight adults. Its first vote lasts one day with the opening voter and candidate lists fixed. Voters may support themselves, revise choices or abstain, and resume ordinary work without volunteering. Three willing candidates with main-round support serve for ten days. Later registrations wait for another contest. Children and other Towns have no vote. ([Town rules](../docs/game-design/towns.md#election-terms-and-council-continuity))
- On the paired world: arrange a cutoff tie. A one-day runoff uses fresh current voters and the original supported tied candidates, preserving settled seats. A remaining tie produces one recorded fair draw. Zero runoff turnout still draws from main-supported candidates. Too few supported willing representatives restores an all-adult council and a one-day retry. ([Town rules](../docs/game-design/towns.md#election-terms-and-council-continuity))
- On the paired world: save before and after the draw, during revised voting, and during a remainder-term replacement. Reload preserves outcomes and deadlines. Departed or deceased residents lose their eligibility; travel alone does not. A change in members or governing form cancels an unfinished proposal, while opening regular voting under the same council preserves it. Proposal votes are final and duplicate requests keep the original window. ([Town rules](../docs/game-design/towns.md#election-terms-and-council-continuity))
- On the paired world: resign a seat and elect a replacement for the original term remainder, with separate consent from full-term willingness. Regular full voting opens one day before term end and takes priority over an unfinished replacement runoff, retaining already settled old-term seats. Representation continues with four to seven adults; at three it returns to the adult council until the Town reaches eight again. ([Town rules](../docs/game-design/towns.md#election-terms-and-council-continuity))
- On the paired world: approach a Hall in a boat. Passengers should land before proposing, nominating or voting, even when the boat is beside the Hall. ([Town rules](../docs/game-design/towns.md#election-terms-and-council-continuity))
