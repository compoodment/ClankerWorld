# Town Hall councils

Pending Windows playtests for the owner's October 1 request and
[approved Town rules](../docs/game-design/towns.md#council-size-and-terms-approved-on-october-1).
This direct owner request has no separate Implementation issue.

- On the paired world: let an adult build the 3×4 Town Hall from actual wood and stone. It should belong to the Town, with residents able to approach it from several sides. ([Town rules](../docs/game-design/towns.md#council-size-and-terms-approved-on-october-1))
- On the paired world: watch a proposal to reserve scarce shared food. Adult council members should attend the Hall before voting; a majority should change the rule, while a tie keeps it. Household goods should keep their owners. ([Town rules](../docs/game-design/towns.md#council-size-and-terms-approved-on-october-1))
- On the paired world: grow one Town to eight adults and watch an election. All its adults should be able to attend and vote without blocking one another, producing three representatives for one game year. Children and residents of another Town should have no vote in that election. ([Town rules](../docs/game-design/towns.md#council-size-and-terms-approved-on-october-1))
- On the paired world: save during voting, then load and continue. Existing votes, Town membership and representative terms should remain intact, and a deceased or departed councillor should lose the seat. ([Town rules](../docs/game-design/towns.md#council-size-and-terms-approved-on-october-1))
