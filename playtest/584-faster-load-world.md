# Faster Load World lists

Pending after merge from [#584](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/584).

- On the paired world: after the server restarts, open Load World. The list may still take a while the first time, but every world appears, and a damaged or incompatible world stays marked as unable to open. ([#584](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/584))
- On the paired world: press Back, then open Load World again. The list appears almost at once, with the same worlds and the same marks as before. ([#584](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/584))
- On the paired world: open a different world from the list, then open Load World again. The world you left appears with its map and can be opened, and opening any world still works as before. ([#584](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/584))
