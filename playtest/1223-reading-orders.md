- Give an adult a real written map, record or book they personally own and carry, then order **Read a map**, **Read a field record** or **Read a book**. The card names the reading task and finishes once that item teaches new written sites; its contents, original discovery sources and other agents’ knowledge stay unchanged. ([#1223](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/1223))
- Name an exact written item in a reading order while that item is on the ground or owned by someone else. The card explains the blocker, another readable item does not replace it, and saving/loading keeps the selected item. Return it to the reader’s personal carried stock; the same task can then finish. ([#1223](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/1223))
- Queue a reading task, enter an unrecognized reading typo, then cancel or replace the task. The typo leaves the queue intact; cancellation or replacement teaches nothing from the cancelled task. A hungry reader takes care of urgent food before reading. ([#1223](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/1223))
