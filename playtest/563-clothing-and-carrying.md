# Clothing and carrying

Prepared for [#563](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/563).
These Windows checks remain pending; automated checks do not replace them.

- In a disposable world, follow a household carrying fiber into its Tailor Shop, weaving cloth and sewing garments. Inspect a wearer: a padded coat should help with cold, and a rain cloak with wet weather. Their map sprite should stay the same. ([#563](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/563))
- Follow rope and basket crafting at a House, and sack crafting at a Tailor Shop. Inspect the agent card: the equipped aid should raise the carrying limit, and actual pickup loads should respect it. ([#563](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/563))
- Follow an agent replacing or repairing worn gear. The old gear and its goods should remain real stock. Breaking an aid may leave a larger existing load, but collecting more should wait until there is room. ([#563](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/563))
- Save during Tailor work, a delivery or a repair, then reopen. Inputs, carried goods, equipped gear and repair progress should remain consistent. Also try a full House or Tailor Shop: refused delivery or production should leave stock intact. ([#563](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/563))
- If a household workshop recipe cannot get its ingredients, check that it pauses so the adult can choose other work, and that its saved plan resumes after supplies return to the building. Reopen the world while it is paused and confirm its plan and progress remain intact. ([#563](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/563))
- Before the first House, let one adult request building material while carrying a full load and another adult carries the requested goods. The helper should meet the requester without transferring while their bag is full. After the requester sets down or uses cargo, the helper should transfer only the amount that fits; save and reopen to check the physical ownership and remaining quantities. ([#563](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/563))
- Let a world run until agents carry maps, tools and materials, then watch a hungry adult whose load is nearly full. They should set down spare supplies with their household (the Event Log says so), then gather food instead of going hungry. Maps and worn gear should stay with them. ([#563](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/563))
