# Clothing and carry aids

Pending Windows playtests for [#563](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/563).

- On the paired world: let a household make rope and a basket at its House, then let an adult equip the basket. The agent should carry more actual goods and unload them into its House when full. ([#563](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/563))
- On the paired world: let the Tailor Shop make a coat, rain cloak and sack from real cloth and rope. Weather protection should come from the garment actually worn, and the sack should fill the single carry-aid slot. ([#563](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/563))
- On the paired world: save while an agent has a filled water jug and carry aid, then load and continue hauling. The jug's water should still count toward the load; removing a carry aid should never discard goods. ([#563](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/563))
