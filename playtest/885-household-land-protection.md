- On the paired world: have one household request or receive farmable land, then watch a farmer from another household choose where to till. The farmer must leave that plot clear while still being able to work free land or their own household's plot. ([#885](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/885))
- On the paired world: try a household land request over another household's existing field. An ordinary request must be refused without changing the field or granting its land; a challenge to an existing recorded use right must remain a dispute. ([#885](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/885))
- On the paired world: request or grant household land beside a growing street or narrow river, then add a nearby building. New Roads, street extensions and bridge entrances must leave the plot clear, while agents can still travel along Roads and bridges that already existed. ([#885](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/885))
