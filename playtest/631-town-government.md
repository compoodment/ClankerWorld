# Town laws, government changes and mayors

- Issue: [#631](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/631)
- Pull request: [#813](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/pull/813)
- Status: awaiting a hands-on Windows check after merge.

Use a disposable world with personal models enabled. Suggest civic actions to
residents; suggestions do not supply their consent or votes. Record the client
and host commits, Windows build, date, resolution and interface size.

- [ ] Ask residents to propose a law, vote, amend it and repeal it. Inspect the
  Towns page for clear scope and effective dates. Confirm the law does not
  silently transfer a building or prevent gathering.
- [ ] Ask residents to create a land mayor. Follow the resident vote, handover,
  willing candidates, ballots and officeholder in Towns and the Event Log.
  Confirm land hearings are described as unavailable.
- [ ] Try a tied mayoral vote and a Council election during mayoral voting.
  Check that the next round and interrupted ballots are explained clearly.
- [ ] Pause during a government vote, save and restart. Confirm the same vote
  and deadline remain, and no authority appears before a valid handover.
- [ ] With separate land and governing mandates, ask the holder to resign one.
  Check the remaining mandate and the vacancy or all-adult fallback shown.
- [ ] Inspect a Town with several laws and past processes at the smallest
  supported window size. Scroll to the last entries and check names and dates.
