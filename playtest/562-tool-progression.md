# Tool progression

Prepared for [#562](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/562). These checks still need a hands-on Windows playtest; automated tests do not replace them.

- In a disposable world, follow an agent gathering loose fallen wood by hand and another felling a tree with an axe. Check that felling wears the axe, provides wood and a tree seed, and that the seed can replant a stump. ([#562](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/562))
- Follow normal decisions through wooden and stone pickaxes, iron ore refinement, and iron tools. Check that finite stone needs a wooden pickaxe, iron ore needs a stone pickaxe, and gold or diamonds need an iron pickaxe; a wrong or worn-out tool should not extract stock. ([#562](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/562))
- Watch household field work and cooking. Compare wooden and iron hoe work and wooden and iron sickle harvests, then try a suitable House recipe with an iron knife. Faster work should use the selected tool and wear it only as progress is committed. ([#562](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/562))
- At a household Blacksmith, follow agents making and repairing tools. Verify a repair consumes real carried materials once, a refusal leaves stock unchanged, and broken tools are replaced with real stock. ([#562](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/562))
- Save during field work, cooking or a repair, then reopen the world. The selected tool, remaining work, inventory, reservations and wear should agree with the saved state. ([#562](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/562))
