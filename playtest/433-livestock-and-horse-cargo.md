# Livestock and horse cargo

Pending Windows playtests for [#433](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/433). No hands-on check is claimed. Acquisition and natural-death rules still await the owner's decision; the checks below need a world with an acquired household herd. Care intervals, yields and cargo capacity are provisional.

- Follow an adult caring for household chickens, a cow or sheep. Feed and water should come from that household's actual supplies, with water carried in a jug, and the adult should visit the animal for its care. Another household should not take its products or use its supplies freely. ([#433](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/433))
- After regular care, watch chickens produce eggs, a cow produce milk and sheep grow wool. Products should stay with the animal until collected; a full-handed adult should leave them there. Sheep shearing should require a usable knife. ([#433](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/433))
- Follow milk into an empty jug and home to porridge, and eggs home to a simple meal. Milk should travel in its jug, should never mix with water already in that jug, and cooking should use the milk while leaving its jug behind. ([#433](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/433))
- Let sheep wool reach the Tailor Shop and become cloth. Once natural death is available, follow an animal's hide through tanning into leather and then into a coat or satchel. The hide should be collected once, and the worn coat or satchel should provide its protection or carrying room. ([#433](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/433))
- Pause an animal's regular care. Its production should stop until care resumes. A horse should stop carrying a rider when its care expires; missed care should not silently kill an animal while that rule is undecided. ([#433](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/433))
- Watch an adult use a cared-for household horse to carry a large load. The rider, horse and loaded goods should move together along a walkable route. Give another named adult riding permission, then withdraw it: their riding should stop while the goods they own remain theirs to retrieve. ([#433](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/433))
- Save and load with an animal waiting for collection, and with goods loaded on a horse. The animal's care, products, rider permissions, jug contents and each cargo owner's goods should remain, and reloading should not create another batch. ([#433](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/433))
