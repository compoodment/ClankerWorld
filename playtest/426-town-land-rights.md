# Town land title and household use

From [#426](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/426).

- Start a new world, accept the first Town layout, and place four founders. Turn on Town land title and household land use. Confirm the first Town's connected border is titled, the two households' assigned building footprints show separate use areas, and the shared Warehouse has no household use color. ([#426](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/426))
- Place an added adult on a clear household use area, then on unclaimed Town land. Confirm the first joins the household with the recorded right, the second joins the Town without a household, and the preview matches the result. ([#426](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/426))
- Build beyond the first Town's accepted border. Confirm the Town border grows while the Town-title overlay stays on the original recorded land. ([#426](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/426))

Filing requests, creating conflicts, and deciding land cases are not available as player actions yet; the striped-dispute view is covered by automated UI and projection checks until that later work lands.
