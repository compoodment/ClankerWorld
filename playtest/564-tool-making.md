# Ask a Blacksmith to make a tool

Pending Windows game checks for the tool-making portion of [#564](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/564). This draft has not yet passed its normal runtime checks or merged; no hands-on pass is claimed.

- Let an adult customer visit a known Blacksmith owned by another household and ask for a tool it does not have in stock. Check that the card names the actual requester and recipe. No goods or payment should move when the request is made.
- Let an adult from the holding household accept or refuse. A fresh personal-model choice should decide; a failed reply, Jev, repeating intention or owner order should not commit either side. A refusal takes nothing.
- Follow accepted work through actual household supply trips. Ingredients at a distant House or another household's workshop must not count as on-site stock. Missing ingredients and full Blacksmith storage should show useful reasons rather than creating materials or a finished tool.
- Watch the ordinary recipe consume the actual inputs and produce one tool at that Blacksmith. The household still owns it. Save and reopen during gathering and production without duplicating work, reservations or output.
- Have the customer return with real payment and propose an exchange for the completed tool. The seller may refuse or ask different terms; the initial request made no price promise. Both traders must meet there before goods move. The customer carries the purchase and payment remains household stock at the Blacksmith.
- Save and reopen with an exchange pending, then accept or cancel it. Check exact goods and payment once, released reservations after cancellation, and the request's final status. Buying grants no private-stock access or household membership.
- Withdraw a request before acceptance and during unfinished work. Payment should remain untouched; existing work, materials and any finished tool keep their owners. Watch interruption if the requester, worker or shop becomes unavailable, without erasing goods or undoing completed work.
- Compare the Blacksmith card, relevant agent note and Event Log. Requester names, recipe, current reason and production-versus-purchase progress should be readable; raw request identifiers, unrelated private stock and model-service payloads should not appear.
- Keep a backup of older worlds. The provisional format is schema 48 above the ornament (47) and Clinic (46) stack; earlier alpha saves are refused and left unchanged.

Market stall use, shared Town construction, currency and new art have their own work and are outside this check.
