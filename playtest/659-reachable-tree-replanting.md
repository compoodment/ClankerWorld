# Replanting island trees

Pending hands-on checks for [#659](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/659).

- On the paired world: create a disposable Small world with seed `island-fuel-review-0` and choose the first Town near `(136,14)`. After an adult fells a local tree, watch them replant a local stump when they choose to do so. The Event Log reports the replanting and the stump becomes a sapling. ([#659](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/659))
- Save and load after replanting. The sapling stays planted and the tree seed stays used up. ([#659](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/659))
