# Tile card, hover label, World Map and filters

Pending for the map panels pull request. No Windows playtest is claimed.

- Click a meadow, a forest tile with a tree, a farm field and a tile with goods on the ground. Each card names the ground, shows a picture of the tile, lists climate as its own row, and fits on screen.
- Move the pointer over land, water and a forest. The label in the corner shows a picture of the ground beside its name.
- Open the World Map in both themes. Towns show a House mark, agents show dots, and the legend reads Town, Agent and Your view.
- Open Filters and switch both on and off. Each row explains what it draws, and the switches look right in both themes and in Settings.
