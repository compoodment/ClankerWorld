# Household shops

Pending paired Windows client/server checks for [#564](https://github.com/compoodment/ClankerWorld/issues/564).

- Watch an adult from another household offer goods for stock physically kept at a Farmhouse, Blacksmith, Tailor Shop or Store. The building card should name the exact exchange and show that both traders must meet there. Until that happens, both original lots stay with their owners.
- Complete the exchange. The buyer should carry the purchase and the selling household should hold the payment at that shop. Buying must not let the visitor take other household goods, cook there or join the household.
- Cancel an exchange, or wait for its offer to expire. The card should show the reason and both traders should retain their original goods without an extra copy.
- Watch a household carry a partial load from its House into a Store. It should remain cargo during the trip and become sale stock only on arrival. Save and reopen during the trip and during an open offer.
- Fill the shop or the buyer's carrying space and confirm an exchange that would overflow it is unavailable. A held offer must keep its receiving space free while other work continues.

Market stalls, tool orders, Restaurants and Clinics are still implementation work in #564 and its dependencies.
